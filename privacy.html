隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 20 日 · 适用于 iOS / iPadOS 版「随心缩」

概述

「随心缩」（以下简称“本应用”）由 PENGYAO 开发，用于在本机调整图片尺寸和压缩文件大小。我们重视你的隐私：本应用不收集、不存储、不上传、不出售也不分享任何个人数据。

我们处理的数据

  • 你选择的图片：仅在你的设备上读取和处理，处理结果保存在设备的临时目录中；不会上传到任何服务器。
  • 应用设置：界面语言和长辈模式开关仅保存在设备本地（UserDefaults）。

我们不做的事

  • 无账号、无登录，不要求填写任何个人信息；
  • 不包含任何统计分析、广告或第三方 SDK；
  • 不追踪你，也不使用广告标识符（IDFA）；
  • 本应用的功能不需要联网，不向我们或任何第三方发送数据。

权限说明

  • 照片（仅添加）：只在你点击“保存到相册”时请求，用于把处理结果存入“照片”。本应用无法读取你相册中已有的照片。
  • 选择图片：使用系统照片选择器或“文件”，只能访问你主动选中的那一张图片，无需完整相册访问权限。

你可以随时在 iOS“设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 照片”中更改或撤销权限。

数据保留与删除

本应用不在服务器上保存任何数据。设备上的临时文件由系统管理，删除本应用即会清除本应用的全部本地数据（包括设置）。

第三方

本应用不与任何第三方共享数据。当你主动使用“分享”时，图片会交给你选择的目标应用，由该应用的隐私政策约束。通过邮件联系我们时，我们只会用你的邮件地址回复你的问题。

儿童隐私

本应用不收集任何人（包括儿童）的个人信息。

政策变更

如本政策更新，我们会在此页面发布新版本并更新生效日期。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026 · Applies to EasyResize for iOS / iPadOS

Overview

EasyResize (“the app”) is developed by PENGYAO to resize images and compress file sizes on your device. The app does not collect, store, upload, sell or share any personal data.

Data the app handles

  • Images you choose are read and processed only on your device. Results are kept in the app’s temporary folder and are never uploaded to any server.
  • App settings (interface language, Elder mode) are stored locally on your device (UserDefaults).

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign‑in, and no personal information is requested;
  • No analytics, advertising or third‑party SDKs;
  • No tracking and no use of the advertising identifier (IDFA);
  • The app’s features work offline; no data is sent to us or to any third party.

Permissions

  • Photos (Add Only): requested only when you tap “Save to Photos”, to add the result to your Photos library. The app cannot read the photos already in your library.
  • Choosing images uses the system photo picker or the Files app; the app can access only the image you select, without full library access.

You can change or revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos.

Data retention and deletion

No data is kept on any server. Temporary files on your device are managed by the system; deleting the app removes all of its local data, including settings.

Third parties

No data is shared with third parties. When you choose to Share an image, it is handed to the app you pick, whose own privacy policy applies. If you email us, your address is used only to reply to you.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
